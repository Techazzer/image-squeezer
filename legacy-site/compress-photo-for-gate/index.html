Frequently asked questions

Why is GATE photo size 240×320 and not 200×230?

GATE uses a portrait (3:4 ratio) photo format while most other exams use a near-square 200×230px format. This is specific to the GOAPS portal used by the IITs. Using 200×230px will get rejected — always use 240×320px for GATE.

My photo is 1MB from the studio. Can I compress it directly?

Yes — upload the original high-resolution file. This tool resizes to 240×320px and compresses to under 80KB in one step. Starting from a larger original gives you better quality at the final compressed size.

Can I use my GATE photo for PSU recruitment?

PSUs that recruit via GATE scores (BHEL, GAIL, ONGC, etc.) typically have their own photo requirements. Check each company's portal, though the specs are often similar (200×230px, 50KB). Keep both versions ready.
