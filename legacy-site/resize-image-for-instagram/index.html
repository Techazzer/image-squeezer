Why does Instagram re-compress my images?

Instagram automatically compresses every image you upload to save bandwidth and storage. This means if you upload a 5MB photo, Instagram will compress it to roughly 200–400KB using its own algorithm — and you have no control over the quality. The result is often visibly blurry or over-compressed images, especially on text-heavy graphics or photos with fine detail.

The fix: pre-compress your image yourself (using this tool at 85% quality) before uploading. Instagram's algorithm will still apply, but since your image is already optimized, there's less headroom for Instagram to degrade it further. Many creators report significantly better final quality using this pre-compression approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Instagram accept WebP images?

No — Instagram only accepts JPEG and PNG. If you have a WebP image (commonly saved by Chrome or downloaded from modern websites), use our WebP to JPG converter first, then resize for Instagram.

What's the best Instagram image size for maximum quality?

Upload at exactly 1080px wide. Going higher doesn't help — Instagram will downsample it anyway. Going lower means Instagram will upsample, creating blur. At 1080px with 85% JPEG quality, you're working with Instagram's native display resolution and the quality will be as sharp as possible.

Should I use JPEG or PNG for Instagram posts?

JPEG for photos. PNG for graphics with text, logos, or flat illustrations. PNG files will be much larger but preserve sharp edges on text and graphics. For typical photography, JPEG at 85% quality gives excellent results and faster upload.
