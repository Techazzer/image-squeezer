Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

🔒 Short version: Aero Tools processes all images entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, stored, or transmitted to anyone. We do not collect any image data.

1. Image Processing

All image compression, resizing, and format conversion on Aero Tools happens using your browser's built-in Canvas API and JavaScript. This means:

  • Your images are loaded into your browser's memory (RAM) only.
  • No image data is transmitted over the internet.
  • No image data is stored on any server or database.
  • When you close the browser tab, all image data is cleared from memory.

This architecture is why Aero Tools is safe for sensitive documents such as passport photos, Aadhaar-linked IDs, exam admit cards, and confidential business images.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII). When you use Aero Tools:

  • No account is required — we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • No image data is collected — your photos never reach our servers.
  • No usage tracking beyond standard web analytics (see §3).

3. Analytics and Advertising

Aero Tools is free and ad-supported. We use:

  • Google AdSense — to display contextual advertisements. AdSense uses cookies to show relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Standard server logs — our hosting provider (Cloudflare) logs IP addresses and request metadata as part of normal web server operation. These logs are retained per Cloudflare's standard policy.

We do not use any first-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) that tracks individual user behaviour.

4. Cookies

Aero Tools itself does not set any first-party cookies. Google AdSense sets third-party cookies for ad personalisation. You can opt out of personalised ads via Google Ads Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Aero Tools is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
